## Rules

- Stick figures, bold black outline, flat fills, zero gradients, shadows, glows, blurs.
- At most 3 poses per character per scene; 5 in a scene with SHAKE or CROSS.
- Cuts are hard unless OUT says color-dip(token). PUSH is the only camera move; CAMERA lock means none.
- Headlines ALL CAPS, top 150 px. Subtitles y 864–1030. Nothing important below y 864.
- Do not add, merge, reorder or drop scenes, and do not add beats that are not on a card.
