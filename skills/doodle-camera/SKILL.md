# doodle-camera

## Frame

- 1920×1080 only. No letterbox bars.

## Zones

- Headline: the top 150 px.
- Subtitles: y 864 to 1030.
- Nothing important below y 864, except the subtitles.

## Framing

- Put eyes on a third of the frame. Do not center every face.
- Leave room in front of every gaze.
- To get closer, cut to a new scene. Do not zoom.

## Camera move

PUSH is the only camera motion (see `doodle-motion`). It goes from 100% to 106% across the scene.
